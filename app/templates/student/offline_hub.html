💡 How Offline Learning Works in Rural Schools
1. At School (Hub Connected)

Connect your phone or laptop to the teacher's Wi-Fi hotspot. Open Offline LMS and tap "Download All Courses".

2. At Home (Zero Internet)

Open Offline LMS anytime. Read lessons, take quizzes, and track your streaks. Submissions are saved locally.

3. Return & Sync

When back in school, connect to the local Wi-Fi. All your scores and completions sync in seconds!
